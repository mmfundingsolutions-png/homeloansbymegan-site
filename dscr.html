What is a DSCR loan?

DSCR stands for debt service coverage ratio. A DSCR loan is a mortgage for investment properties that qualifies you on the property’s cash flow instead of your personal income. The lender asks one main question: does the rent cover the payment?

That makes DSCR loans a fit for investors whose tax returns don’t tell the whole story: self-employed owners with big write-offs, investors with several properties, and anyone who’d rather not hand over two years of returns for every purchase. They’re sometimes called no-income-verification investor loans or rental income loans.

How DSCR is calculated

The ratio compares the property’s monthly rent to its full monthly housing payment, called PITIA: principal, interest, property taxes, insurance, and any HOA dues.

DSCR = monthly rent ÷ monthly PITIA

A DSCR of 1.0 means the rent exactly covers the payment. Many programs look for 1.0 or higher, and a stronger ratio often means better pricing. Some programs accept less, usually with more money down.

For a purchase, rent usually comes from the appraiser’s market rent schedule. For a refinance, it can come from your current lease. For short-term rentals, it may come from rental history or a market projection, depending on the program.

Typical DSCR loan requirements

Every program is a little different. These are the things lenders look at:

DSCR

Often 1.0 or higher. Some programs go lower with more money down.

Down payment

Many purchases need at least 20–25% down. Cash-out refinances usually have a lower maximum loan-to-value.

Credit and reserves

Minimum scores vary by program, and higher scores usually price better. Expect to show several months of payments in reserves.

Appraisal and rent schedule

Confirms the property’s value and market rent. If you’re closing in an LLC, you’ll also provide entity documents.

Prepayment penalty

Common on DSCR loans, typically for one to five years. A shorter penalty usually means a higher rate.

DSCR loan questions, answered

What is a DSCR loan?

A DSCR (debt service coverage ratio) loan is a mortgage for rental and investment properties that qualifies you based on the property’s rental income instead of your personal income. There are no tax returns, W-2s, or pay stubs to document. The lender compares the property’s rent to its monthly housing payment to decide whether the property supports the loan.

How is DSCR calculated?

DSCR equals the property’s monthly rent divided by its monthly payment, including principal, interest, property taxes, insurance, and any HOA dues (PITIA). For example, $3,200 in rent divided by a $2,500 payment is a DSCR of 1.28. A ratio of 1.0 or higher means the rent covers the payment.

What DSCR do I need to qualify?

Many programs look for a DSCR of 1.0 or higher, and a higher ratio often means better pricing and loan-to-value. Some programs allow a ratio below 1.0, usually with a larger down payment and a higher rate. Requirements vary by program.

Do DSCR loans require tax returns or proof of income?

No personal income documentation is required. There are no tax returns, W-2s, or pay stubs. Lenders still review your credit, your reserves, the property appraisal, and a rent schedule or lease.

How much down payment do I need for a DSCR loan?

Down payment depends on the program, your credit, the DSCR, and the property type. Many DSCR purchases require at least 20 to 25 percent down, and cash-out refinances usually have a lower maximum loan-to-value than purchases.

Can I get a DSCR loan in an LLC?

Yes. DSCR loans commonly allow you to close in the name of an LLC or other business entity, usually with a personal guarantee from the owners.

Can I use a DSCR loan for an Airbnb or short-term rental?

Many DSCR programs allow short-term rentals. Income may be based on the property’s rental history or a market rent projection, depending on the program. Local short-term rental rules, including San Diego’s permit requirements, still apply.

Can I use a DSCR loan for my primary residence?

No. DSCR loans are business-purpose loans for investment properties only. They can’t be used for a home you or your family live in.

Do DSCR loans have prepayment penalties?

Many do. A prepayment penalty is a fee if you pay off or refinance the loan within the first few years. Common structures last one to five years, and a shorter penalty usually means a higher rate. I’ll show you the options so you can match the penalty to how long you plan to hold the property.

Can I get a DSCR loan for a property outside California?

Yes. I originate DSCR loans for rental properties in most states, so San Diego investors can finance rentals out of state as well as in California.
